Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Newely is an interview practice app provided by BENC Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16806878), whose registered office is at 167-169 Great Portland Street, London, England, W1W 5PF ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller of your personal information.

Newely helps you prepare for job interviews using AI-generated questions and AI-powered feedback. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Newely mobile app. If you have any questions, contact us at ben@benclabs.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive basic profile information from that provider (such as your name and email address, depending on what you choose to share) so that we can create and identify your account. We use Firebase Authentication to manage sign-in.

You can look around Newely without an account. We collect no personal information from you until you sign in.

Purchase and usage information

Newely gives each account 3 free answers, then offers one-off passes (3, 7 or 30 days). Passes are single payments, not subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple, and we never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to record purchases and grant your pass; it receives your account ID and your purchase details. On our own backend we store your display name, your pass status and expiry date, and your usage counts (the number of answers you have submitted for feedback, including in the last 24 hours).

We use your email address to send account emails: a welcome message, a pass confirmation and an account deletion confirmation.

Content you provide

  • Job details. If you fill in a company and role, or paste a job description, that text is used to generate interview questions for that role.
  • Your answers. When you answer a practice question by typing, that text is saved as part of your practice history.
  • Video and audio recordings. If you choose to record a video answer, the app uses your device's camera and microphone (with your permission) to capture it.
  • Practice history. For each completed session we save the company and role, the interview stage and difficulty, the questions, your answers (typed or transcribed), and your scores and feedback.

Automatically collected information

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across other apps or websites. Standard technical information, such as error logs, may be collected to help us diagnose and fix problems.

Age requirement

Newely is for people aged 16 and over. By creating an account you confirm that you are 16 or over. See section 7 for more on children's privacy.

2. How we use AI, and what happens to your data

Newely uses third-party AI providers to power its core features. This is what is sent, to whom, and what happens to it.

Generating interview questions

The job description, company name, role, interview stage and difficulty level you choose are sent to Anthropic (maker of the Claude AI model) to generate realistic interview questions and answer hints for your situation.

Transcribing your spoken answers

If you record a video answer:

  • The video is uploaded from your device to our secure backend (hosted on Google Firebase Cloud Functions).
  • Our backend extracts the audio and sends it to OpenAI (maker of the Whisper transcription model) to turn your spoken answer into text.
  • The video and the extracted audio are deleted immediately after transcription, from your device and from our backend. We do not keep, store or reuse your recordings. Only the text transcript is kept, as part of your practice history.

Biometric information

A video answer may briefly contain biometric identifiers such as your voice or face. We use it only to produce a text transcript. We do not extract, store or use voiceprints, faceprints or any other biometric template, and the recording is never used to identify or profile you. Because recordings are deleted immediately after transcription, no biometric data is kept beyond that process. If you live somewhere with specific biometric privacy laws (such as Illinois), this also serves as our statement that we do not retain biometric data.

Judging your answers

The text of your answer (typed, or transcribed from video), together with the question and its hint, is sent to Anthropic's Claude model to produce a score, a category breakdown and written feedback.

Automated scoring

Scores and feedback are produced automatically by an AI model, without human review. They are a practice aid, not a formal or official assessment, and should not be treated as a definitive judgement of your interview ability. If you think a score is inaccurate or unfair, you can disregard it, try the question again, or contact us at ben@benclabs.com.

Notes on third-party AI processing

  • Anthropic and OpenAI process this data on our behalf as service providers, under their own data processing and API terms.
  • At the time of writing, neither provider uses data sent through its API to train its models by default. Each may keep API inputs and outputs for a limited period (for OpenAI, up to 30 days) for abuse and safety monitoring, after which they are deleted unless the law requires longer. We encourage you to read Anthropic's and OpenAI's privacy policies for details.
  • We do not send these providers more information than is needed to generate your questions, transcribe your answer or produce your feedback.

Legal basis for processing (UK and EU users)

  • Account creation and sign-in: performance of a contract (our Terms of Use).
  • Passes, usage limits and account emails: performance of a contract, and our legitimate interest in managing purchases and preventing misuse.
  • Generating questions and feedback, and saving your practice history to your account: performance of a contract, and our legitimate interest in providing the app's core features.
  • Camera, microphone and clipboard access: your consent, given when you choose to use those features. You can withdraw it at any time (see section 4).
  • Technical and error logs: our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the app.
  • Responding to legal requests: compliance with a legal obligation.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Your practice history (questions, answers, scores and feedback) is stored on our backend (Google Firebase) and linked to your account. This lets you see your history again after reinstalling the app or changing device. It is accessible only to your account through the app, and access rules limit each account to its own data.
  • Your display name, pass status and expiry, and usage counts are also stored on our backend (Google Firebase).
  • Video and audio recordings are never stored (see section 2).

International data transfers

Our backend (Firebase and Google Cloud) and our service providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, RevenueCat and Resend) may process data on servers outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK and EU data protection law, such as Standard Contractual Clauses and, where they apply, adequacy decisions, so that your data keeps an equivalent level of protection. You can ask for more detail by emailing ben@benclabs.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit, restricted access to backend systems, per-account access rules on your practice history, and prompt deletion of recordings after transcription. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Your choices and controls

  • Delete a practice session. Available from your session history at any time. It is permanently removed from your account.
  • Clear all practice history. Available from the menu at any time. This permanently removes all your saved sessions.
  • Delete your account. Available from the menu at any time. This permanently deletes your sign-in, your profile record (display name, pass status and usage counts) and all your saved practice sessions. Any remaining pass time is lost and cannot be moved to another account. Apple and RevenueCat may keep a record of your purchases, and we keep a record of pass purchases (see section 8), as required for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Restore purchases. Your pass is linked to your Newely account, so signing in again on a new device or after reinstalling restores it automatically. If it does not appear, use "Restore purchases" on the pass screen.
  • Camera and microphone. You can decline or withdraw this permission in your device settings at any time and still use Newely by typing your answers.
  • Clipboard. Used only if you tap "Paste from clipboard" when adding a job description. We do not read your clipboard at any other time.

5. Third-party links

Newely provides shortcuts to search for job postings on external sites (such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Reed and Glassdoor). They open in your device's browser, are run independently of Newely, and are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising networks or share your data with advertisers. We share information only with:

  • Anthropic and OpenAI, as described in section 2, solely to provide the app's AI features.
  • Firebase and Google Cloud, which provide the sign-in, database and backend that Newely runs on, and which store your account and practice history.
  • Apple and Google, whose sign-in services you use to create your account, under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat, which records purchases and helps us grant your pass.
  • Apple, which processes App Store purchases and payments.
  • Resend, which delivers our account and purchase emails.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where required by law.

Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation, insolvency or sale of some or all of our assets, your information may be disclosed or transferred as part of that transaction. If your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy as a result, we will tell you through the app or by other reasonable means.

7. Children's privacy

Newely is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe someone under 16 has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. How long we keep data

  • Recordings (video and audio): never kept. Deleted immediately after transcription (see section 2).
  • Practice history (questions, answers, scores, feedback): kept on your account until you delete the session, clear your history or delete your account.
  • Account information and profile record (display name, pass status and expiry, usage counts): kept until you delete your account, then deleted.
  • Pass purchase records: when a pass is bought we keep a record of your account ID, which pass was bought and when it ends. We keep it for accounting, fraud prevention and resolving purchase disputes, and it is not deleted when you delete your account. Apple and RevenueCat also keep purchase records under their own policies.
  • Technical and error logs: kept on our backend for up to 30 days, only to diagnose and fix problems, then automatically deleted.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your practice history and your account yourself in the app (see section 4). For anything else, including a copy of your data, email ben@benclabs.com. We aim to respond within one month.

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). If you are in the EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • know the categories of personal information we have collected about you and why (see sections 1 and 2);
  • delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, so no opt-out is needed; and
  • not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at ben@benclabs.com. We may need to verify your identity first.
